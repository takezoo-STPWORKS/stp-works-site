
# Privacy Policy for "Old Coin Pusher Friends 2" (Android and iOS)

> This English version is a reference translation. The Japanese version is the original; if there is any inconsistency, the Japanese version prevails, except where the laws of your country or region require otherwise.

STP WORKS Co., Ltd. ("we," "us," or "STP WORKS") sets out below how we handle users' information in "Old Coin Pusher Friends 2" (the Android and iOS versions; the "App"), a game app for smartphones and tablets, and in the online features we provide through the App (together with the App, the "Service").
How we handle information on our website is governed by the privacy policy for our website.

## 1. Information We Collect

The App collects the following information.

| Type | Details | How it is collected |
|---|---|---|
| Player name | The name you set in the App (if you do not set one, "Player" followed by 4 digits) | Entered by you |
| Identifiers | A random ID that the App creates on your device, a player ID issued by the server we use, and a temporary value created each time the App starts | Created and sent automatically by the App |
| Game records | Rating, total play time, the type of device you play on (Android/iOS), and the game status while you play online (room, passphrase, stickers sent, and actions such as firing coins) | Sent automatically by the App |
| Records of suspected cheating | When the App detects a change in game speed or rewriting of memory: the type of detection, the date and time, and the version of the App | Sent automatically by the App |
| Communication information | IP address, the date and time of communication, and your approximate location, such as country and city, estimated from your IP address when you log in | Collected automatically as part of communication |
| Transfer information | When you choose "Show transfer code" in the App: a copy of your game records (encrypted on your device) and a value created from the transfer code (the transfer code itself is not sent) | Your actions |
| Records of operator measures | Instructions from us to change your player name or correct your rating under the Terms of Use, and the date and time the App applied them | Set by us and sent automatically by the App |
| Report information | When you report another player: that player's player name, that player's player ID (if you reported from the leaderboard), the reason for the report, where you made the report (leaderboard or room) and the room name, the date and time, and your player name | Your actions |
| Records of deletion requests | When you ask for account deletion from the App: the date and time, your player name, device type, and App version | Your actions |
| Inquiry information | Email address, the content of your inquiry, and your in-game ID and verification code | Email from you |

The App does not send any information to us or to the companies in Section 4 until you agree to the Terms of Use and this Policy when you first start the App.
The App does not collect your real name, address, phone number, date of birth, device location (GPS), contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, advertising ID, or device-specific identifiers. Payment for the App is handled by the stores (Google Play and the App Store), and we do not receive payment information.

## 2. Purposes of Use

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

1. To provide the Service (creating and identifying accounts, saving game records, data transfer when you change devices, and determining which device is valid when the same account is used on more than one device)
2. To provide features for playing online together with other users
3. To compile and display the leaderboard shared by the App and the other versions (the Steam version and the Nintendo Switch version)
4. To prevent, investigate, and respond to cheating and violations of the Terms of Use (including measures such as correcting ratings and suspending use)
5. To investigate and fix bugs and to improve the Service
6. To respond to inquiries and to confirm your identity
7. To give important notices, such as changes to, interruption of, or termination of the Service
8. To respond as required under laws and regulations

We do not use the information we collect for delivering advertising or for marketing. We also do not sell users' information.

## 3. Information Shown to Other Users

1. Your player name, rating, and total play time are **shown on the leaderboard to all users of the App and of the other versions (the Steam version and the Nintendo Switch version).**
2. Other users playing online with you (including users of the other versions) can see your player name, the stickers you send, and the game status.
3. The name of a room for playing with a passphrase includes the passphrase. Passphrase rooms created in the App are not shown in the room list, but the passphrase may become known to other users through people who join the same room or through rooms created in the other versions. Please do not use your own or anyone else's personal information in your player name or passphrase.

## 7. Retention Periods

| Information | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Player name, identifiers, game records | Until the account is deleted |
| Records of suspected cheating | Until the account is deleted. However, for accounts against which we have taken measures under the Terms of Use, we keep the account's identifiers and the records of the reasons for 1 year after the account is deleted, to prevent repeated cheating |
| Communication information (login records, IP address, approximate location) | Until the account is deleted |
| Report information | Until the account of the user who made the report is deleted. However, if we have taken measures under the Terms of Use based on the report, the same period as "Records of suspected cheating" above |
| Records of deletion requests | Until deletion is completed (deleted together with the account) |
| Transfer information | Until the transfer is completed on the new device (deleted at that time). If you do not transfer, until the account is deleted |
| Records of operator measures | Same period as "Records of suspected cheating" above |
| Inquiry information | 3 years after we finish responding |
| Game records on your device | Until you delete the App, or until you ask for deletion of your account and data from the App (we cannot delete records on your device remotely) |

If we end the online features of the Service, we will delete the information in this table that we keep (except inquiry information and records of measures taken under the Terms of Use) within 6 months after they end.

## 8. Deleting Your Account and Data {#delete}

You can ask us to delete your account and the information we store about you in either of the following ways:

1. **From the App**: Go to "Delete Account & Data" in "Settings".
   The game records on this device are deleted immediately, and the next time you launch the App, you will start with a new account. Records on our servers are deleted as described in the next paragraph.
2. **Without using the App** (for example, after you have deleted the App): Make a request by email to support@stp.works, including the "in-game ID" and "verification code" shown in the App's "Settings" and your player name. If you do not know your in-game ID, please tell us your player name and the approximate date and time you last played. We will check the information you give us against our records to confirm that the request is from you.

Within 30 days of receiving a request, we will delete the account and the player name, game records, records of suspected cheating, login records, and transfer information linked to it, and also remove them from the leaderboard (except for records that Section 7 says we keep after deletion). We will also delete the records held by the companies we entrust with handling information (Section 4). Deleted accounts and game records cannot be restored.

If you wish to stop information from being sent to the Service, please also delete your account in this way and delete the App from your device.

## 9. Security Measures

We take the following measures to prevent leakage, loss, or damage of the information we collect:

1. Basic policy: We have established this Policy for the proper handling of information.
2. Handling rules: We have set out the methods of, and the persons responsible for, collecting, using, storing, and deleting information.
3. Organizational measures: We have appointed a person responsible for handling information and have set up a system for reporting when we become aware of facts, or signs, of handling that violates laws or this Policy.
4. Personnel measures: We make sure that employees who handle information are aware of the precautions for handling it.
5. Physical measures: We prevent theft and loss of equipment that handles information, and we control the areas where information is handled.
6. Technical measures: We limit who can access the administration screens, and we take measures to prevent unauthorized access from outside. The App's game records are encrypted when stored on your device.
7. Understanding the external environment: When entrusting companies located outside Japan with handling information, we take security measures based on an understanding of the personal information protection system of that country (the United States) (Section 5).
